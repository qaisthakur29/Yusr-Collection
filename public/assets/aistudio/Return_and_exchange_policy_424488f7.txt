🔄 Returns & Exchange Policy 
Heading: RETURNS & EXCHANGE POLICY
Sub-text: Please read carefully before ordering. We want you to love your gear.

Content:
At Yusr Collection, every single piece is custom-printed specifically for you (Made-to-Order). Because of this, we follow a strict but fair policy aligned with our printing partners (Qikink).

1. Size Exchange (Allowed):
If the size doesn't fit you as expected, we offer a one-time size exchange.

Condition: The exchange request must be raised within 48 hours of delivery. The product must be unused, unwashed, and have all original tags intact.

Shipping: The customer will bear the shipping cost to send the product back to our warehouse. We will cover the shipping to send the new size to you.

2. Damaged or Defective Product (Free Replacement):
If you receive a damaged, torn, or misprinted item, we will replace it for free.

Mandatory: You must record a clear video while opening the package (Unboxing Video) showing the shipping label and the defect.

Without an unboxing video, we cannot process replacement claims for damages.

3. No Refunds for "Change of Mind":
We do not offer monetary refunds. We only offer size exchanges or replacements for defects. Please check our size chart carefully before ordering.

4. How to raise a request?
WhatsApp us at +91 9326184407 with your Order ID and the Unboxing Video (if applicable) within the specified time frame.

(Note: No physical address is displayed as we operate via our centralized printing and fulfillment partners to keep costs low and quality high).
