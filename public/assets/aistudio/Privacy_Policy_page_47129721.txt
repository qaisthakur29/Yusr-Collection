4. 🔒 Privacy Policy Page

Heading: PRIVACY POLICY

Content:
At Yusr Collection, your privacy is our priority. This policy explains how we collect and use your data.

Information We Collect: When you place an order, we collect your name, shipping address, email, and phone number.

How We Use It: We use this information solely to process your order, ship the product to you, and send order updates via WhatsApp/Email.

Payment Security: We do not store your credit/debit card or UPI details. All payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways (like Razorpay) which comply with PCI-DSS standards.

Third-Party Partners: We share your shipping address with our printing and courier partners (Qikink) strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your order.

Marketing: We will never sell or rent your data to third parties. You can opt out of our promotional messages at any time.
